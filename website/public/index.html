Chrome extension · free

Keep your tabs sorted by what you're working on

Groupify lives in Chrome's side panel. Put every new tab in one group with a click, sort them again by task or purpose, and reopen groups you closed.

Add to Chrome

  • Group as a seriesEvery ungrouped tab in one new group, named for you.
  • ReclassifyBy task, purpose, site or last use, or just say how you want them. Undo puts it back.
  • Lock, save, reopenLocked groups stay put; closed groups wait under Recently closed.
  • Private by defaultYour tabs stay on your computer. Cloud AI is optional and off by default.
